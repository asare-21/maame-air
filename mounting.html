    Mounting and cover¶

    The board has three hole patterns. Coordinates are in mm from the board's front-left corner, x to the right and y towards the rear.

    Holes Pattern Size Positions (x, y) Use
    H5–H8, "FRAME M2.5" 48.5 mm square Oval slots 4.2 x 2.7 mm (23.25, 34.0) (71.75, 34.0) (23.25, 82.5) (71.75, 82.5) Board to the S500 centre plate
    H1–H4 30.5 mm square 3.2 mm (M3) (32.25, 43.0) (62.75, 43.0) (32.25, 73.5) (62.75, 73.5) Kakute H7 stack
    Pi holes 58 x 23 mm 2.75 mm (M2.5) (17.93, 90.53) (75.93, 90.53) (17.93, 113.53) (75.93, 113.53) Pi Zero support

    • To the frame: four M2.5 standoffs, at least 10 mm, through the frame slots. The slots are oval so they can line up with the frame's holes. Trim all through-hole leads to 1.5 mm or less underneath so nothing touches the plate. The two rear frame slots sit under the edge of the Pi, so fit their screws before the Pi.
    • Kakute: on its own rubber grommets with M3 screws through H1–H4. Its arrow must point at the nose, matching the silkscreen arrow ("FRONT = KAKUTE ARROW").
    • ESC cable slot: a 14 x 5 mm obround cut-out centred at (50.5, 47.6), inside the Kakute square. The ESC's 8-pin cable comes up through it from the lower plate. A 65 mm cable reaches.
    • Pi Zero: plugs into U1 face down; its component side sits about 11 mm above the board (8.5 mm socket plus the header's spacer). If you add M2.5 standoffs at the Pi holes, measure that gap first. No carrier parts sit under the Pi, and the tallest Pi part (mini-HDMI, about 3.2 mm) leaves about 7.8 mm.

    Moisture cover¶

    A 3D-printable lid is in the project folder under maame/cover/: MaameDrone_V1.0_cover.stl (ready to print), .step for CAD and the .py source.

    • Size: 102 x 126.5 x 36.6 mm outside, 30 mm clear inside. The skirt hangs 3 mm below the board so water drips off the edge.
    • Print: PETG or ASA (PLA softens in the sun), roof down, no supports, about 60 g. White or natural filament lets the status LEDs glow through the 0.6 mm roof panel.
    • Mount: swap the 4 nuts on top of the Kakute for M3 male-female standoffs, then fix the lid with 4 x M3x14 screws and rubber washers. Standoff length = 22 mm minus the height of the Kakute's screw tops above the board.
    • Cable exits: open slots for J3, J8, J11, J13 (left) and J9, J10 (right). J20, J12, J6, J17, J18 and J7 have 0.6 mm knock-outs; cut one out only when you use that header.
    • Sealing: close cable gaps with foam tape or silicone, but don't make it airtight, which traps condensation. For damp conditions also conformal-coat the board, masking the headers, Pi socket, fuses and JST socket.

    Unconfirmed

    The lid assumes the Kakute stack is under 21 mm and the Dupont plugs with their cable bend are under 20 mm tall.
