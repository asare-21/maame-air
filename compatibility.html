    Compatibility¶

    The board is built around a Pi Zero W v1.1 and a Kakute H7 V1.5. Other parts work if they match the footprint, voltage and signal listed here. "Tested part" means designed and checked against datasheets; nothing has been flown on this board yet.

    Companion computer (U1 socket)¶

    Board Fits Notes
    Raspberry Pi Zero W v1.1 Yes, the design target Mounts face down. Its 2x20 male header must be on the component side (as on a Zero WH).
    Raspberry Pi Zero WH Yes Header is already on top, which is what the face-down mount needs.
    Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W Yes Same 65 x 30 mm outline, 58 x 23 mm holes and 40-pin pinout. Faster (quad core), and draws more power, up to about 0.6 A busy; the 5 V rail has the headroom.
    Raspberry Pi Zero / Zero 1.3 (no wireless) Yes Works electrically; no Wi-Fi for setup or data download.
    Pi Zero with the header soldered underneath No, as is Pins would point away from the socket. Use a Pi with the header on top.
    Pi 3, 4, 5, Pi 3A+ and other full-size boards No Different size and hole pattern. The Pi 5 also maps its UARTs differently.

    The socket must be the standard 8.5 mm tall 2x20 female header (LCSC C5124634) so the face-down Pi sits at the designed height.

    Flight controller (J1, J23, J24, J25)¶

    • Holybro Kakute H7 V1.5: the design target. The four header rows copy its pad names, so the Dupont harness runs straight across.
    • Kakute H7 V1 / V1.2: same 35 x 35 mm size and 30.5 mm M3 holes. The bottom 6-pin connector's pin order differs on V1.2 drawings, so check pad labels before wiring. V1 uses a BMP280 baro; flash the same KakuteH7 target.
    • Kakute H7 v2 and Kakute H7 Mini: they fit the holes, but the pad layout and firmware target differ. You would need your own harness map.
    • Any other 30.5 x 30.5 mm M3 flight controller: it mounts, because the FC is joined by a wire harness rather than plug-to-plug. Map each header pin to that FC's pads by function (5V, GND, UART TX/RX, SDA/SCL, servo out).

    ESC¶

    The ESC is not on this board. The Tekko32 F4 65A (AM32) sits on the S500's lower power plate. Its 8-pin cable comes up through the slot in the carrier straight to the Kakute's ESC port. Any 4-in-1 ESC with a JST-SH 8-pin cable that the Kakute supports works the same way. The board only taps battery voltage from the ESC pads (J3).

    Peripherals¶

    Function Tested part Also works Watch out for
    GPS + compass (J8) TBS M10Q with Q5883 compass Any 5 V GPS with UART + I2C compass (e.g. Matek M10Q-5883, Holybro M10) J8 order is TX RX GND VCC SCL SDA, matching the TBS pads. Lead colours vary by maker; wire by function, never by colour.
    Telemetry radio (J9) SiK Telemetry Radio V3, 433 MHz, 100 mW Any 5 V UART radio (915 MHz SiK, other MAVLink radios) UK law limits 433 MHz to 10 mW: set the radio's TXPOWER to 10. High-power radios (RFD900x) can draw close to 1 A and should not run off 5V_FC.
    RC receiver (J10) FlySky FS-iA10B on iBUS Receivers with iBUS, SBUS or PPM on one signal wire J10 carries only the FC's R3 input. Two-way protocols that need a TX line (CRSF, ELRS telemetry) won't send telemetry back through it.
    Rangefinder (J11) Benewake TF-Luna, I2C mode (CFG grounded), address 0x10 Other 5 V I2C lidars ArduPilot supports Shares the I2C bus with the baro and compass. A miswired I2C device stops the baro.
    Payload servo (J13 + Kakute M5) 9 g micro servo Any 5 V servo under about 1 A stall Power comes from 5V_AUX, never from the FC.
    Camera None fitted Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 or v1.3 with a 15-to-22-pin Zero cable The carrier has no camera mount. The Pi's camera connector is on its short edge opposite the SD card, facing the board. Check the latch can open before you commit to a camera.

    Battery¶

    Designed for a 6S pack, 18 to 25.2 V (Li-ion or LiPo). The battery tap fuses and TVS are rated 30 V, so packs above 6S are not supported.
