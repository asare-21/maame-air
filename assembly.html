    Assembly and bring-up¶

    Build the board, prove the 5 V rail on a bench supply with nothing plugged in, then add the Kakute, the Pi and each peripheral one at a time.

    Soldering the board¶

    1. Check the JLC-assembled board: look for bridges on U2's pins and check that D8, D9 and C1 sit the right way round.
    2. U1 Pi socket: tack one pin at each end, check it sits square and flat, then solder the rest. A tilted socket stops the face-down Pi seating.
    3. Kakute headers J1, J23, J24, J25, then the peripheral and spare headers you need (J8, J9, J10, J11, J13; J6, J7, J12, J17, J18 optional), then the J20 JST socket.
    4. J3 battery wires: 18 AWG, red to "+", black to "−". Leave the other ends free for now.
    5. Trim every lead underneath to 1.5 mm or less.

    Kakute harness¶

    1. Solder 26–28 AWG wires to the Kakute pads named in the J1, J23, J24 and J25 tables.
    2. Leave B+, Vo, Vi, CAM and 9V unwired. They have no net on the board, and B+ on a signal pin would destroy parts.
    3. Crimp the other ends into Dupont housings in the same order as the headers, pin 1 to the square pad.
    4. Before plugging in, beep each wire from the Kakute pad to its header pin. Check the J1 rows aren't swapped: pin 1 is B+ (unwired), pin 2 Vo (unwired), pins 3 and 4 GND.

    First power-up¶

    1. Bench supply on J3: about 20 V, current limit about 0.3 A, nothing plugged into any header. The AUX5V LED should light.
    2. Measure J6 pin 1 or J13: 5.0 to 5.1 V. If it is wrong, stop and check U2 and R10/R11 before going further.
    3. Kakute: plug in the harness, then power the Kakute as normal (USB or ESC). The FC5V LED lights when the Kakute's 5 V rail is up; whether USB alone powers that rail is unconfirmed. Measure 5 V on J10 pin 2.
    4. Pi: check the orientation twice (pin 1 at the right, rear row; SD right; USB/HDMI rear), then plug it in face down and power from J3. The Pi boots from 5V_AUX.
    5. Peripherals, one at a time, checking each in Mission Planner before the next: GPS (J8), then receiver (J10), then SiK (J9), then TF-Luna (J11), then servo (J13 power + Kakute M5 signal).
    6. Then connect J3 to the ESC's battery pads and do the full system check on the real battery: Pi ready message, arming gate, LEDs, and a servo and motor test with the props off.

    Tip

    If the barometer reports "unable to initialise" after you add an I2C device, unplug the device: a wrong SDA/SCL lead holds the bus and stops the baro.
