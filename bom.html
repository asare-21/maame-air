    Bill of materials¶

    JLCPCB assembles the 32 surface-mount parts (21 BOM lines). You buy and solder the through-hole parts yourself. LCSC stock was checked on 28 Oct 2026. The fab files are fab/MaameDrone_V1.0_BOM_JLC.csv, fab/MaameDrone_V1.0_CPL_JLC.csv and fab/MaameDrone_V1.0_BOM_hand_solder.csv in the board repo.
