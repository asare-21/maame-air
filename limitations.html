    Limitations and open items¶

    The board has not been built or flown yet. These points are unconfirmed or are design limits to know about.

    Unconfirmed¶

    • Kakute 5 V current limit. Everything on 5V_FC (GPS, SiK, receiver, TF-Luna, spares) shares it. Keep high-current loads on 5V_AUX.
    • Kakute pad pitch against the J1/J23/J24/J25 header layout. Check with a 1:1 print before crimping the harness.
    • Lead order of your SiK and TF-Luna cables, and the M10Q's wire colours. Wire by the labels on the device, never by colour.
    • Cover fit: Kakute stack height (under 21 mm assumed) and Dupont plug height (under 20 mm assumed).
    • Camera clearance: the carrier has no camera mount, and nobody has checked that the Pi's camera connector latch can open with the Pi face down.

    Design limits¶

    • 6S only (18 to 25.2 V). The fuses and TVS are rated 30 V.
    • No on-board enable or low-voltage cut-off for the 5 V buck: it runs whenever the battery is connected.
    • No 9 V or 12 V outputs (removed in V1.0 to cut cost). The Kakute's 9V pad is not brought to the board.
    • J10 has only the R3 receive line, so two-way RC protocols can't send telemetry back through it. T3 is available on J17 if needed.
    • The Pi's GPIO16, 18, 19, 20 and 21 drive the status LEDs, so they aren't free for other uses.

    Small mismatches in the design files¶

    • The J3 schematic value says 20 AWG; use 18 AWG, as the assembly notes and BOM say.
    • The values of J1, J23, J24, J25 and U1 still say "(JLC THT)". Since the hand-solder change they are not fitted by JLC.
